About Amos Clothing

Bringing quality fashion to Kenya, Ghana, and beyond

Our Story

Amos Clothing & Accessories was founded with a simple vision: to make quality, stylish clothing and accessories accessible to everyone across Africa. What started as a small online store based in Bomet, Kenya, has grown into a cross-border fashion destination serving customers in both Kenya and Ghana.

We noticed that many shoppers in East and West Africa struggled to find affordable, trendy clothing without long waits or high shipping costs. So we built a store that combines quality products, fair prices, and fast delivery — with payment options that work for real people, like Mobile Money and cards.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, high-quality fashion to customers in Kenya and Ghana, delivered conveniently to their doorstep, with secure and easy payment options that fit their everyday lives.

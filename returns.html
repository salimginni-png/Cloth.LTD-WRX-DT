Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

1. Our Promise

At Amos Clothing & Accessories, customer satisfaction is our priority. If you are not happy with your purchase, we offer a straightforward return and refund process. Please read this policy carefully before requesting a return.

2. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet the following conditions:

  • Return requested within 7 days of delivery
  • Item is unused, unworn, and unwashed
  • Item is in its original packaging with all tags attached
  • You have proof of purchase (order confirmation or receipt)
  • Item is not on our non-returnable list (see Section 3)

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons:

  • Underwear and swimwear
  • Earrings and pierced jewelry
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance"
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items damaged due to customer misuse

4. How to Request a Return

Follow these simple steps to start your return:

1

Submit Request

Fill out the return form below or email us within 7 days of delivery.

2

Get Approval

We will review your request and respond within 24–48 hours.

3

Ship Item Back

Send the item to the address we provide, in original packaging.

4

Receive Refund

Once we receive and inspect the item, we process your refund.

5. Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected:

  • We will notify you by email of the approval or rejection
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method (Mobile Money or card)
  • Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the item was damaged or incorrect

6. Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size or color:

  • Follow the same return process described above
  • Indicate the desired exchange item in your request
  • Exchanges are subject to product availability
  • Price differences (if any) must be paid or will be refunded

7. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you received a damaged, defective, or incorrect item:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery
  • Provide photos of the item and packaging
  • We will cover the return shipping cost
  • You will receive a full refund or free replacement

8. Return Shipping Costs

Shipping costs for returns are handled as follows:

  • Customer's responsibility: Change of mind, wrong size ordered, or personal preference
  • Our responsibility: Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

We recommend using a tracked shipping method for returns, as we cannot process refunds for items lost in transit.

9. Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund within 10 business days:

  • Check your mobile money or bank account again
  • Contact your payment provider, as processing times may vary
  • If you still haven't received it, contact us at kipkiruiamos001@gmail.com
